Basis

Cable data in use

LV single-core ratings, resistance, reactance and K come from the AS/NZS 3008.1.1 tables you supplied: Tables 3.13, 3.14, 4.5(A), 4.1(A), 5.1 and 5.2, with earth sizes from AS/NZS 3000 Table 5.1. LV multicore and flexible SDI ratings come from manufacturer data based on AS/NZS 3008.1.1. 11 kV ratings come from the Prysmian MV guide. The manufacturer data will be replaced as more AS/NZS 3008 tables are supplied.

Method

1. Design current

3-phase: I = P / (√3·V·pf), or S / (√3·V). 1-phase and DC: I = P / (V·pf), or S / V. With parallel runs, each run carries I / n (assumes identical cables of equal length).

2. Current rating

Iz = Itab × ktemp × kgroup × ksoil × kdepth × kother × n. The temperature factor is ktemp = √((θmax − θ) / (θmax − θref)), with θmax = 90 °C (X-90 / XLPE), θref = 40 °C in air and 25 °C in ground. Pass if Iz ≥ Ib, and also Iz ≥ In when a device rating is entered (AS/NZS 3000 cl. 2.5.3).

3. Voltage drop

  • LV single-core (AS/NZS 3008 data): the operating temperature is estimated as θo = θa + (θmax − θa)(Irun/Iz,run)². R is interpolated at θo from Table 4.5(A). X comes from Table 4.1(A): trefoil, or flat for the touching and spaced installations; XLPE for X-90 and elastomer for 110 °C cables. Then Vd = √3 (3-phase) or 2 (1-phase/DC) × Irun × L × Zc / 1000, where Zc = √(R² + X²) (conservative) or R·cosφ + X·sinφ.
  • LV multicore (manufacturer data), 3-phase: Vd = Vc × Irun × L / 1000, using the tabled 3-phase Vc (mV/A·m at 90 °C, which is conservative). LV AC 1-phase: Vc × 1.155.
  • 11 kV: Vd = √3 × Irun × L × Zc / 1000, using Rac,90 and X.
  • DC: Vd = 2 × Irun × L × Rθ / 1000, where Rθ = R20(1 + 0.00393(θ − 20)) and the operating temperature is estimated as θ = θa + (90 − θa)(Irun/Iz,run)².

4. Short circuit (adiabatic)

Smin = If·√t / K. K comes from AS/NZS 3008.1.1 Table 5.1 (copper, final temperature 250 °C for X-90 and cross-linked elastomers per Table 5.2), interpolated at an initial temperature equal to the estimated operating temperature θo. Parallel runs are assumed to share the fault current (n·S ≥ Smin). For 11 kV the manufacturer's 1 s conductor and screen ratings are scaled by 1/√t.

5. Earth conductor

The minimum earth size comes from AS/NZS 3000 Table 5.1: actives of 400–630 mm² take ≥ 120 mm², and actives above 630 mm² take ≥ 25 % of the active size. The earth is then checked adiabatically against the earth fault current, using the same K as the actives.

6. Earth fault loop impedance (LV AC)

Maximum length by the AS/NZS 3000 Appendix B simplified method: Lmax = 0.8·Uo·Sph·Spe / (Ia·ρ·(Sph + Spe)), where ρ = 22.5×10⁻³ Ω·mm²/m (copper) and Uo is the phase-to-earth voltage. Parallel runs use n·S. For generator-supplied circuits the 0.8 source factor may not hold, so check the generator's fault contribution separately.

Limitations

  • Aluminium is available for LV single-core cables (Tables 3.13 and 3.14) and for 11 kV cables (Prysmian). Its resistance comes from IEC 60228 until AS/NZS 3008 Table 4.5(B) is supplied. Aluminium-active circuits get a copper earth from the AS/NZS 3000 Table 5.1 aluminium column. Loop impedance uses ρ = 36×10⁻³ for aluminium actives.
  • LV multicore cables still use manufacturer ratings and tabled Vc until AS/NZS 3008 Table 3.4 (multicore XLPE) and the multicore impedance tables are supplied.
  • Grouping, soil thermal resistivity and depth of laying factors are entered manually until the AS/NZS 3008 derating tables are loaded.
  • 2-core and DC circuits use the 3/4-core multicore ratings (two loaded conductors would rate slightly higher, so this is conservative).
  • Cable types with no "enclosed" column (flexible cables and 11 kV) are rated for a lidded tray as the touching or free-air rating × the covered-tray factor. The default 0.80 is the enclosed/touching ratio seen in the LV multicore data. Replace it with the AS/NZS 3008 value.
  • The 11 kV ratings are taken from Prysmian's tables. Their rating conditions aren't stated in the extract used and are assumed to be the AS/NZS 3008.1.2 reference conditions (40 °C air, 25 °C ground). Confirm these.
  • Not covered: harmonic derating, motor starting voltage drop, cyclic ratings or solar exposure (except the LV multicore "exposed to sun" column).
